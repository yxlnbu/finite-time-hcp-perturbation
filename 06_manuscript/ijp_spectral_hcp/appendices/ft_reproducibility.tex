The corresponding scripts include \path{build_ijp_operator_consistency_v2.py}, \path{build_ijp_strengthening_evidence_v1.py}, \path{build_ijp_revision_evidence_v3.py}, \path{run_ijp_singular_vector_nonlinear_validation_v3.py}, \path{run_ijp_v3_dense_convergence_v1.py}, \path{audit_ijp_v4_full_fourier_convergence_v1.py}, \path{audit_ijp_v4_start_branch_metric_v1.py}, \path{build_ijp_material_parameter_provenance_v1.py}, \path{build_ijp_reproducibility_release_v1.py} and \path{build_ijp_core_figure_set_v1.py}. The numerical gates require: (i) the conditioning limits in Eq.~\eqref{eq:dyn_conditioning_gates}; (ii) exact finite/infinite descriptor root counts; (iii) finite-root and reconstruction errors below registered tolerances; (iv) convergence under independent base-history and propagator refinement; (v) less than 10\% change in $t_c$ under registered factor-two scale tests; (vi) like-for-like full-state comparison and invariant-projection positive-control equality below $2\times10^{-11}$; (vii) stability across the registered norm--selector matrix and three orientations; (viii) selector-specific direction--wavenumber re-optimization with boundary maxima classified rather than converted into wavelengths; (ix) the formal singular-value separation gate in Eq.~\eqref{eq:dyn_singular_degeneracy_gate}; (x) convergence of the same-$\boldsymbol P_{\rm in}/\boldsymbol P_{\rm out}$ piecewise-frozen control; (xi) independent spatial, temporal and integration-format convergence of the dealiased nonlinear replay; (xii) less than 1\% discarded nonlinear right-hand-side energy before complete harmonic closure is claimed; and (xiii) explicit failure labels for unmatched experimental landmarks, unresolved nonlinear spectral flux and seed-dependent widths. A failed gate disables the corresponding interpretation rather than replacing it with a regularized value.

The local publication-ready archive now contains the complete complex optimal right/left singular vectors, all 129 generator checkpoints for every reported baseline branch, full-state and projected operator receipts, the norm--selector matrix, mechanism histories, branch-start metadata, every optimizer evaluation, nonlinear Fourier fields and the executable environment manifest. The strict HCP-to-Bai analytical reduction, an independent global-optimum proof, coordinate-invariant $t_c$, an experimental observation covariance, energy-metric direction--wavenumber re-optimization, experimental onset validation, an identified dislocation free energy, propagation resistance and material band-width identification remain false claim gates. The search evidence applies only as an anchor-assisted basin-retention audit on the registered compact domain, anchors, tolerances and discretization ladder stated in Section~\ref{sec:numerical_dynamic}.
