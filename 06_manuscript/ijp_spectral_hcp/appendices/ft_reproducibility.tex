\item \path{cp_ti_dynamic_descriptor_qz_v1.json}: 87-coordinate generalized Schur audit, finite/infinite root classification, complete finite-root matching and regularization-denial gates;
\item \path{cp_ti_finite_time_dynamic_perturbation_v2.json}: base trajectory, operator contract, coordinate scales and original direction--wavenumber scan;
\item \path{cp_ti_continuous_spectrum_robustness_v2.json}: 129-state propagation reference, continuously refined branches, temporal convergence, norm sensitivity, principal-symbol checks and sampled tail;
\item \path{cp_ti_finite_time_discrimination_v1.json}: historical projected-gain diagnostic retained for traceability and not used for the primary like-for-like subtraction;
\item \path{ijp_operator_consistency_v2.json} and \path{ijp_operator_consistency_v2_arrays.npz}: unified full-state propagator/frozen comparisons, projected positive controls, logarithmic-norm bounds, commutators, modal condition numbers, eigenvector rotations and the complete norm--selector sensitivity matrix;
\item \path{ijp_strengthening_evidence_v1.json}: stationary/commuting-normal controls, three-orientation transfer and an anchor-assisted projective-sphere--wavenumber basin-retention audit with complete optimizer trajectories;
\item \path{ijp_strengthening_evidence_v1_arrays.npz}: all complex 69-by-69 generator checkpoints, propagators, input/output singular vectors and singular spectra for every reported baseline branch, plus three-orientation generators and complete global samples;
\item \path{cp_ti_mechanism_causality_v2.json}: all 64 fixed-base cross-coupling coalitions, exact Shapley allocations and temporal-reorganization diagnostics;
\item \path{hcp_qs_spectrum_verification_seed_v1.json}: independent frozen quasi-static gradient controls;
\item \path{cp_ti_fwhm_seed_spectrum_v1.json} and \path{cp_ti_lsbrp_q3_nonautonomous_bridge_v1.json}: same-window seed/spectrum/amplitude matrix, linear transport checks, grid convergence and nonlinear material-scale denial;
\item \path{ijp_singular_vector_nonlinear_validation_v2.json}, \path{ijp_singular_vector_nonlinear_validation_v2.csv} and \path{ijp_singular_vector_nonlinear_validation_v2_fields.npz}: complete nonlinear periodic validation of the actual near-onset and terminal right singular vectors, including complex output-vector, amplitude-collapse, Fourier-leakage and energy-ledger gates;
\item \path{ijp_core_figure_set_v1.json}: one receipt connecting every core figure to source artefacts and explicit claim boundaries.

\end{enumerate}

The corresponding scripts include \path{build_ijp_operator_consistency_v2.py}, \path{build_ijp_strengthening_evidence_v1.py}, \path{run_ijp_singular_vector_nonlinear_validation_v2.py}, \path{build_ijp_material_parameter_provenance_v1.py}, \path{build_ijp_reproducibility_release_v1.py} and \path{build_ijp_core_figure_set_v1.py}. The numerical gates require: (i) the conditioning limits in Eq.~\eqref{eq:dyn_conditioning_gates}; (ii) exact finite/infinite descriptor root counts; (iii) finite-root and reconstruction errors below registered tolerances; (iv) convergence under independent base-history and propagator refinement; (v) less than 10\% change in $t_c$ under registered factor-two scale tests; (vi) like-for-like full-state comparison and invariant-projection positive-control equality below $2\times10^{-11}$; (vii) stability across the registered norm--selector matrix and three orientations; (viii) anchor-assisted nested-search coverage, basin recurrence, interior $k^*$ and same-resolution reference comparison; (ix) complete nonlinear reproduction of the onset and terminal singular-vector predictions; and (x) explicit failure labels for unresolved or seed-dependent nonlinear widths. A failed gate disables the corresponding interpretation rather than replacing it with a regularized value.

The local publication-ready archive now contains the complete complex optimal right/left singular vectors, all 129 generator checkpoints for every reported baseline branch, full-state and projected operator receipts, the norm--selector matrix, mechanism histories, branch-start metadata, every optimizer evaluation, nonlinear Fourier fields and the executable environment manifest. The strict HCP-to-Bai analytical reduction, an independent global-optimum proof, coordinate-invariant $t_c$, experimental onset validation, an identified dislocation free energy, propagation resistance and material band-width identification remain false claim gates. The search evidence applies only as an anchor-assisted basin-retention audit on the registered compact domain, anchors, tolerances and discretization ladder stated in Section~\ref{sec:numerical_dynamic}.
